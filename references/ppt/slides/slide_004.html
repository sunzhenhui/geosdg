01 · Background

Three Gaps in Existing Approaches

Gap 01

Retrospective

Existing scores rely on historical statistics and lack dynamic simulation of future trends and policy interventions.

Gap 02

Non-Spatial

Frameworks like iSDG, IFs, SDG-IO, T21 are statistics-driven, ignoring spatial variability and process-driven dynamics.

Gap 03

Single-Domain

Existing CA models simulate land use only — no framework yet couples it with infrastructure dynamics critical for accessibility indicators.

A framework that is prospective, spatially explicit, and dual-domain is still missing.
